This is where I think out loud. The topics range from geopolitics to philosophy to cybersecurity to the economics of collapse, often in the same article. If that sounds scattered, it is not: I believe the most interesting insights happen at the intersection of domains, not inside them.

**A note on process.** The ideas, analysis, and positions in these articles are mine. They grow out of real conversations, daily research, and lived experience. The writing is produced collaboratively with AI (Claude by Anthropic), working from extensive conversation transcripts and source material. I use AI the way a previous generation used a good editor: to structure, clarify, and sharpen thinking that already exists. I believe in full transparency about this, because hiding the process would contradict everything I write about.
